## What Was Delivered

### 1. The AI assist module (`src/modules/ai-assist/` — the 20th §28 module)

- **`classify`**: the candidate universe is the market's LIVE visible taxonomy tree (§13/§14/§35 — `getPublicTree`); the model ranks the top-3 nodes for a draft with one-sentence reasons. It can never invent a node outside the tree.

- **`dedup`**: a pg_trgm `similarity()` pre-filter narrows the universe to the 5 nearest units by canonical title (the §17 engine family — measured cost control), then the model judges each: likely-duplicate or not, with a reason (§7 — one canonical unit per piece of knowledge; duplicates fragment mappings and mastery).
- **The standing AI constraints, all structural**: the z-ai model layer (`ai-model.ts`) is SERVER-ONLY and OFF the module barrel — imported exclusively by the route (the P9-S1/P9-S3 SDK-leak class, found live twice); every response carries the §26 contract line; nothing is auto-applied; model failures leave no partial state (clean 503s with honest messages).
