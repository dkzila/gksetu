## Key Decisions

1. **One §35 chain per module**: the inventory reuses the dashboard's `resolveLabelMarket` (now exported) — the controls page labels EXACTLY what the dashboard labels, through the same lenient-for-derived / strict-for-explicit chain.
2. **Effect sentences mirror the queue reasons verbatim** (§9 coherence): the inventory's "Units under this subject rise up your queue and carry the reason 'Because you follow the subject X'" quotes the exact sentence the dashboard renders — the explanation surface and the recommendation output share one vocabulary, so a user can cross-check them.
3. **Per-signal removal refs are data, not UI** (§39): each follow row carries `{method: 'DELETE', path: '/api/follows/{id}'}` — any client (web, future app) renders its own controls from the API without hardcoding. Goal rows deliberately carry none: the goal is ONE coherent declaration (§9) replaced wholesale — its controls are the group-level Edit (the guided flow / PUT) and Remove (DELETE /api/goal).
4. **The reset scope is exactly the §9 signals**: follows + goal + onboarding back to PENDING (the fresh canvas — the guided flow may offer itself again, and skip remains honest). Saves/collections are structurally OUT (§10 — the receipt and the reset contract say so, and the §31 test proves saves survive); account identity fields stay in the profile self-service (resetting a home country would silently break the §14 guards).
5. **Idempotent + audited**: resetting an empty account is a success with zeros (the follow/onboarding precedent); the mutation records ONE `user.personalisation.reset` audit entry with before/after counts and the kept counts in metadata (§30 — the trail records mutations, reads stay unaudited).
6. **§36 honesty on the controls surface**: an INACTIVE/RETIRED exam keeps its row with an honest effect sentence ("stays listed here but leaves your queue until it is active again") — the P5-S4 feed behaviour, stated where the user manages it.
7. **The howItWorks block is computed, not static**: every sentence carries live counts (2 goal exams, 1 followed exam, 1 saved item, home market IN) — the standing explanation can never drift from the user's actual state.
